# My Favorite Hobby: Web Browsing
**Web Browsing**: The action of searching the web, or "surfing", simply for fun.

---
## Why I Love It
Web Browsing is simple, yet fun. Usually I go on my favortie sites on my computer to potentially discover something I didn't know before.
## My Gear
- My Chromebook **or** my Cellular device.
- A known Wi-Fi source.
- Links to some of my favorite sites. (Unless I want to go to a fresh, new site I haven't already discovered.)
- A web browser, usually [Google](https://www.google.com/?safe=active&ssui=on).
## Tips For Beginners
When Web Browsing,  cleaning up certain customizable sites ([Gmail](https://mail.google.com/mail/u/0/), [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/u/0/r?pli=1), etc. Products other than Google can also be used, usually depending on browser/computer software.) can really help. Go to your settings and make sure to clean up certain things!
